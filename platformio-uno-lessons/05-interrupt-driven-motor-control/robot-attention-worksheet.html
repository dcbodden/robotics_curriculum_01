Follow the plan and keep checking

Walking across a room, you follow a plan and watch for chairs or people. Robots need fresh information too: has a control changed? Is the path clear? Is the motor too hot? Checking often can help avoid collisions and damage.

Today we check the RV control. Obstacles and heat would need other sensors.

Lesson 04: Check, act, and wait

The loop() repeats five steps. Find these code pieces in lesson 04:

analogRead(ANALOG_INPUT_PIN) 1. Read. Get the RV reading on A0: 0 to 1023.
map(adcReading, ADC_MIN, ADC_MAX, PWM_MIN, PWM_MAX) 2. Map. Change the reading to a power command: 0 to 255.
analogWrite(MOTOR_PWM_PIN, pwmCommand) 3. Apply. Send the PWM power setting to the motor circuit on D3.
Serial.print(adcReading);
Serial.println(pwmCommand);		 4. Report. Show both numbers on the computer.
delay(CONTROL_UPDATE_DELAY_MS); 5. Wait. This delay value is 1000 milliseconds: one second.

The wait is blocking: the loop does not read the RV again during it. The motor keeps receiving its old PWM power setting.

Move the RV just after a reading: the command can lag by about one second. Move it just before the next reading: the wait is shorter. An old “go” command could keep a robot moving toward a chair.
